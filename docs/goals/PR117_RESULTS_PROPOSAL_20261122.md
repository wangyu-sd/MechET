## Figure 3. Lead with action-level credit, then test whether the weights learned it

Each comparison joins two genuinely executed actions at an identical input to a stock-closing and a failed continuation. The independently replayed inputs give 38 pairs with different mapped successors. Of the 30 pairs with distinct electron-flow successors, the instantaneous teacher favored the failed action in 19. The historical weight of 0.5 scales the observed +0.5 and −0.5 advantages to +0.25 and −0.25. Twenty-eight of the 40 comparisons occurred after a prior reaction commit.
