## Figure 3. Lead with action-level credit, then test whether the weights learned it

The first 100 optimizer updates contain 40 observed historical comparisons among 195,768 learner action groups. Each comparison joins two genuinely executed actions at an identical input to a stock-closing and a failed continuation. The independently replayed inputs give 38 pairs with different mapped successors. Of the 30 pairs with distinct electron-flow successors, the instantaneous teacher favored the failed action in 19. The centered local learner advantage favored it in 20, and adding the historical term reversed all 20 of those pair preferences before clipping. The historical weight of 0.5 scales the observed +0.5 and −0.5 advantages to +0.25 and −0.25. Twenty-eight of the 40 comparisons occurred after a prior reaction commit. The exact target-level replay and all nominal margins are available with the figure. [Complete learner census and both action coefficients](https://github.com/wangyu-sd/MechET/pull/117#issuecomment-7084826839)

Through update 117, the learner has 46 observed outcome comparison groups among 229,026 candidate groups. Across 35 FLOW pairs with distinct executed successors, all 24 adverse local preferences reverse on the two executed samples alone. When local and weighted history coefficients are summed across all samples of each grounded action, 22 of 23 adverse preferences reverse. The update-117 exception has two identical completions for each action. Its margin changes from −0.294 to +0.206 on the executed samples and from −0.587 to −0.087 on all duplicate samples. These are nominal differences before policy ratios, clipping, KL, and the shared route term. [Grouped credit and exception](figures/pr117_grounded_action_credit_through117.pdf)
