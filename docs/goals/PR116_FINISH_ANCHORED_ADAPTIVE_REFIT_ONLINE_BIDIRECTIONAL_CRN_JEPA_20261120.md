# PR116 — FINISH-Anchored Adaptive ReFIT-PO with ONLINE bidirectional CRN-JEPA

**STATUS: NEW USER-APPROVED PRIORITY / IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATION · 2026-11-20.**
**This document supersedes the fixed-K=4-at-every-node schedule in** [PR116 Stepwise K4 historical design](PR116_STEPWISE_REFIT_PO_K4_HISTORICAL_CREDIT_20261120.md). **The original frozen PR116 completed 64-update evidence, PR110/D3 job histories, and stopped online FlowER experiments remain unchanged. This PR update is design/docs ONLY, not a claim that new training, code, or speed results exist.**

## 0. Main decision

Keep **ReFIT-PO**, real policy-generated native **IMPORT/FLOW/FINISH** decisions, the already-trained Qwen3.5-0.8B Stage-II History-89442 actor, current Executor, source D3 multi-step training corpus, AND/OR stock closure and genuine route-level RL. Do not switch to FlowER multi-step SFT, offline DPO-only, template-only planning, or a fresh pretrained actor. No online FlowER teacher.

**New cost-saving sampling contract:**
- Ordinary decisions **K=1** sampled native action. When invalid, ambiguous, promising to compare, or previously failure-prone, **add one candidate** (K=2); optionally expand a few difficult states to **K=4 maximum**, within a per-target *additional-action-request* cap. K is **adaptive**, not fixed-four.
- Score eligible sampled **FLOW proposals ONLINE, at the decision state, before choosing/committing the next action**, with the frozen **CRN-JEPA PR18** checkpoint using BOTH authenticated directions. The evaluator must be synchronous with action selection **for an uncached scored FLOW**, although inference requests are batched across all ready states and equivalent results cached. It is **not** post-FINISH-only or a delayed background teacher.
- On every accepted **FINISH**, immediately check the completed reaction edge against a TRAIN-only reaction index, deliver bounded local **reference/precedent reward**, and propagate that reward to causally contributing FLOW + FINISH tokens in the actual reaction. No need to generate a full suffix just to learn that this one authentic reaction matched.
- Keep a bounded stream of **real full multi-step searches** to stock closure for long-horizon credit; interleave many cheap 1–2 FINISH training segments that terminate as **segment-complete / continuation-unknown**, not falsely stock-closed/chemical-failed. On a genuine successful full AND/OR route, backtrace the actual executed electron actions and compute additional terminal reward. Reuse historical genuinely failed alternatives for same-state contrast without inventing new receipts.
- Train on **D3 source TRAIN broadly** and schedule distinct target roots / diverse difficulty, not the earlier 2,048-root-only result; source TRAIN references provide hidden scoring and synthetic reachable evidence only, never policy prompt labels.

## 1. Real-time dual-direction CRN-JEPA event scoring (MANDATORY, not optional delayed scoring)

**Verified PR18 source:** [CRN-JEPA PR18](https://github.com/wangyu-sd/CRN-JEPA/pull/18) implements `DirectionalCRNPretrainFull`, a pretrained Graph Transformer with explicit `chemical_forward` and `retrosynthetic_inverse` direction contexts, grounded `GroundedMove`, native action teacher-forcing, and a verified trained/EMA checkpoint with SHA-256 `bb2f7fe53e87fc0e2438c27065ceb588dcd38e604959c9d69fb3984885a376ee`. The PR18 Event@K is *development evidence of directional event modeling*, **not** a measured synthesis success rate or a pre-existing integrated MechET real-time serving API. **Build the adapter and measure actual latency.**

For a proposed native executable inverse event `a_inv`:

1. Snapshot current **inverse-planning before state** `S_before`; compile model-issued native FLOW via existing MechET `compile_event_arguments` / grounded event adapter. First ensure Executor accepts the move on a temporary state, yielding `S_after`. If not executable, do not give it positive neural credit.
2. **Inverse-side score:** run frozen PR18 grounded event likelihood of `(S_before, a_inv, direction="retrosynthetic_inverse")`, i.e. `s_inv = -NLL_PR18(event | S_before, inverse)`, using exact legal source/sink/CONTINUE/COMMIT factors of the PR18 grounded decoder and consistent event cardinality normalization.
3. **Forward-side score:** construct the **chemically correct inverse operator**, applied on the actual `S_after` as `a_fwd`, with `direction="chemical_forward"`. Verify this explicit forward replay returns the same `S_before` graph under the canonical chemistry state contract (including mapped identity, charges and stereo); then obtain `s_fwd = -NLL_PR18(a_fwd | S_after, forward)`. Importantly **do not just swap source/sink tokens**, do not label inverse moves as forward, and do not use original `S_before` as the forward starting state. For a multi-move event, construct the reversed, ordered forward operator with the actual grounded move semantics; if any step is inexpressible or fails replay, mark `forward_score_status=unavailable` rather than invent a score.
4. Compute a normalized **two-direction local electronic evidence score** `s_bidir = w_inv*z_inv(s_inv) + w_fwd*z_fwd(s_fwd)` with direction-specific frozen train-only calibration. Keep `s_inv`, `s_fwd`, eligibility flags, event cardinality, replay status and `s_bidir` **separate** in all saved records. Prefer a simple equal-weight starting condition (0.5/0.5) for eligible pairs; when one direction is not scoreable, use the valid one but log its coverage; no false penalty for an unavailable score. Never interpret neural values as calibrated probability of experimentally feasible chemistry.
5. The online controller uses this score to decide **which of the currently sampled legal inverse FLOW options to execute** and for a bounded local reward after executing; it can also trigger a second sample when neural evidence is low or close / uncertain. Illegal moves remain illegal irrespective of PR18 ranking. Native IMPORT and FINISH are **not** ElectronEvents and are scored by Executor/reference semantics, not forced through CRN.
6. **Batch/latency contract:** load checkpoint once per scorer worker, inference mode/no grad, batch scored FLOW candidates from many active targets; cached by canonical mapped state + canonical grounded event + direction + checkpoint hash + executor contract + calibration version. One K1 FLOW can require two directional scorer evaluations (one batchable score request containing two examples), **but zero extra Qwen token-generations**. Report actual PR18 calls, batch size, GPU-seconds and critical-path waiting rather than treating the scorer as free. Aim for a single worker/low overhead and reuse features where possible.
7. Implementation: reuse `src/crn_jepa/models/pr18_full_corpus_world.py` and `src/crn_jepa/models/pr18_full_corpus_objectives.py:teacher_forced_from_tokens` (source/sink/commit CE). Match training-time normalization, atom-map reindexing, availability/inventory and direction labels. Compare scalar likelihood implementation against the existing teacher-forced objective on a small fixed native event batch in both directions before GPU rollout.

**One electron transition**: `Qwen proposes -> native Executor validates temporary successor -> frozen PR18 scores inverse(S_before,a_inv) + forward(S_after,a_fwd) ONLINE -> stochastic selection -> commit selected successor`. Neural reward is bounded relative to the verified Executor result and cannot manufacture endpoint success.

## 2. Immediate per-FINISH reference reward and preserved multi-step RL

On successfully accepted `finish_trace`:
- Materialize the actual completed reaction edge: `product -> unordered multiset(precursors)` using project’s existing isotope/charge/stereo-preserving canonical identity, `edge_signature`, and exact precursor multiplicity. Validate productivity and ancestry/cycle constraints.
- Query TRAIN-only `references.sqlite` / `route_occurrences` and a newly built immutable **TRAIN global edge index**. Distinguish:
  - **task/path supported:** the committed edge belongs to an authenticated TRAIN reference route for the root and matches a reachable **trusted reference prefix/occurrence**; strongest local positive evidence;
  - **global TRAIN reaction precedent:** the same chemistry appears in some other D3 TRAIN route, but is not necessarily a valid continuation of this target's reference path; smaller positive evidence, never count as root reference proof;
  - **executor-valid novel edge:** allowed, but reference support UNKNOWN; no fabricated invalidity.
- Give the actual committed reaction an **immediate bounded, non-duplicated progress credit**; redistribute a declared fraction to the **FLOW actions that causally produced the net electronic edit** (reuse `route_flow_credit.py`), remainder to FINISH. Keep one credit per completed edge occurrence and cap repeated/symmetric/replayed loops. Example suggested values only: +0.15 target/path reference, +0.05 global TRAIN precedent, 0 novel, with bounded total process reward per root. Do not confuse these with validated chemical yield or final synthesis success.
- **Unlike current first-rejection terminal rollback**, a later rejected action should NOT erase already observed genuine successful FINISH chemistry. Record the later failure and negative/terminal long-horizon credit separately. Prevent exploitable repeated one-step collection by unique progress accounting and truncation controls.
- A completed FINISH only ends **one reaction**. Push all precursor occurrences into pending AND obligations; stock precursors are discharged, non-stock precursors remain. **Only a genuinely executed complete, productive, acyclic AND/OR synthesis with every terminal precursor in stock gets terminal all-stock reward**. Merely reaching reference-reachable states in a static TRAIN lookup is **not** model stock closure.
- Build a CPU-only TRAIN reference reachability index over frozen reference AND/OR route trees for optional **training-only potential shaping** at the moment an actual committed FINISH matches an appropriate precursor frontier; distinguish "reference-reachable" vs "actor-generated stock-closed". Never leak this lookup through Qwen prompt or TEST/VALID scoring. Preserve real full-chain terminal reward as dominant.

## 3. Adaptive K1/K2/K4 and exact sampling receipts

Default **K=1** per exact native decision (IMPORT, FLOW or FINISH). Maintain full model-generated token and behavior logprob receipt; execute candidates on temporary snapshots. Do not add G4=4 independent complete routes per target nor fixed additional full ReFIT suffixes.

Escalate to **K=2** only when: a proposal is rejected; known historical same-state failure exists; scorer reports a bounded, empirically low-quality event; competing legal actions are valuable to compare; or local uncertainty suggests multiple paths. Expand up to **K=4** in selected hard states **only within a capped extra-generation budget per root**. Keep legal unchosen branches for direct replay/backtrack. Do not force all four or require a second Qwen call at every FINISH. Use **epsilon/temperature stochastic selection** among real legal proposals; select even at K1 with probability 1. Cache executor results; cached chemistry cannot fabricate a fresh policy receipt.

Credit rules:
- **K>=2:** genuine distinct same-conditioning sampled action comparisons can use local group-centered Executor + PR18 score advantages and genuine observed continuation comparisons. Keep receipt identity unique, duplicates not additional independent chemical votes.
- **K=1:** use legitimate process/return-to-go policy gradient with a task-level or running baseline, not a fictitious group-size-four GRPO baseline. In cases without an informative baseline, still record and train supported positive/negative process examples via the declared estimator.
- Full-route success and failure propagate **only through actually executed policy decisions** in their real trajectories, with the original ReFIT same-state credit when actually observed; unexpanded sibling continuation outcome UNKNOWN, never copied success or failure.
- Conditional slate-and-selection behavior must be logged (native proposal probabilities, selector probabilities, branch policy version) and used consistently in the RL estimator; no silent equivalence of score-selected routes to raw independent Qwen rollouts. If sampling only K1, original actor distribution and behavior logprobs remain native.
- Prior-policy historical failures are valuable to avoid resimulation and for declared off-policy contrast, but not treated as fresh current-policy samples.

## 4. Training schedule: broad D3 coverage without forced full-route suffix cost

Source: **D3 reaction-holdout n1–n5 TRAIN = 156,723 source route rows**; VALID 8,250. These are *source route rows*, not 156,723 independent distinct targets. The target-only policy sees only actual target and owned executed observations. Preserve all true multi-step reference trees in private scorer DB; read unique TRAIN canonical-root count from the actual D3 manifest before scheduling. Where a target appears with multiple TRAIN references, combine their actual route trees as multiple independent known references, not a spurious hybrid target-route.

Training mix initial proposal (modifiable by observed effective signals, not claimed completed): ~80% cheap **segment RL**, stop after 1–2 actually accepted FINISH commits (or failure), and ~20% **full AND/OR route RL** with actual terminal stock closure / genuine rejection. For sampled segment cuts, mark `segment_complete_continuation_unknown` not `stock_solved` nor `rejected_action`. Terminating after reference-supported FINISH is a **training collection boundary**, not an inferred solution; do not penalize the unresolved chemistry. Reuse exact real action receipts; no invented suffix likelihoods.

Coverage: span the complete D3 source TRAIN with target dedup, chemistry/difficulty stratification and explicit sample-budget schedule. Favor meaningful unique target/edge coverage rather than mechanically finishing 156,723 separate expensive full routes; preserve their reference data. Do not postpone starting meaningful policy updates until a full D3 epoch. Collect finished reference-supported short segments and legitimate long complete routes in each update.

For a bounded full-route budget, store success certificates, rejected decision contexts and alternate legal actions. No fixed 16 (or 2) entire extra suffix rollouts; backtrack by resuming retained snapshots only as needed. Full training continues to yield real long-horizon supervision at manageable cost.

## 5. Calls/cost and expected receipts (do not present projections as completed measurements)

**Observed PR116 first 16 updates on 512 TRAIN roots:** 139,568 actual Qwen action-generation requests total; 48,075 old independent G4 calls and 91,493 extra suffix calls; mean **272.6 Qwen requests / root**. One independent G4 route averaged about **23.5** calls / root route.

**New K1-adaptive cost model (planning estimates only):**
- `N_Qwen = N_live_decisions + N_extra_candidates + N_backtrack_new_decisions`. FINISH reference matching and history lookup are CPU-only; online dual-direction PR18 incurs GPU scoring but **does not multiply Qwen generations**.
- Illustrative normal full-route 25–40 Qwen requests/root yields **85.3–90.8% fewer** Qwen-generation calls than observed PR116, if similar route lengths and restrained backtracking.
- Illustrative 1–2 FINISH segment 12–20 requests/root yields **92.7–95.6% fewer** than observed PR116, **but these are shorter tasks with less long-term coverage**; not matched-route-quality speedups.
- Do **not** promise 12–20 or 25–40 until observed distributions of first accepted FINISH length, rejection rate, K escalation rate, segment success, and total PR18 online latency are measured. For 156,723 source rows each with one 12–20-request segment, rough 1.88–3.13 million Qwen requests **before** separate full-route collection; if deduplicating targets actual roots and calls change. Report full method total model compute incl PR18, Qwen prefill/decode tokens, learner time and route closure.
