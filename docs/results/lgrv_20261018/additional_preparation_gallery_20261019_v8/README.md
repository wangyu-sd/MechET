The [review specification](../../../../configs/analysis/lgrv_checked_preparations_20261019_v1.json)
records independently transcribed named structures, actual preparation
locators, conditions and short source witnesses. Source bytes, normalized
text, all nine exact reaction keys and frozen-query membership were checked
before drawing. Methods referenced in another example were read explicitly;
their substrate-specific quantities/yields were not silently transferred.
Crude masses and source OCR inconsistencies are labelled. Unspecified imine
or ring stereochemistry is not upgraded to an assigned configuration.

All nine PDF/SVG/PNG exports passed183-mm/maximum170-mm geometry, vector,
embedded-font and300-dpi checks and were rehashed. All three actual PNGs
were opened and inspected for structures, charges, labels, conditions and
clipping. Older galleries and original PR87 figures remain untouched.

These are **forward net-reaction precedents** for transformations generated
by inverse electronic construction. Their straight scheme arrows are not
electron-flow arrows and do not validate each symbolic intermediate or a
whole route. Source-positive gallery selection cannot estimate population
chemical accuracy or matched-compute superiority. Full patent text remains
outside Git; citations/metadata and short witnesses are distributed here.
