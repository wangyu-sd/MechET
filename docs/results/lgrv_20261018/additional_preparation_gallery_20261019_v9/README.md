# Five additional source-checked preparations

All five newly checked exact generated reactions are drawn with RDKit on two
183-mm vector/PDF plates with300-dpi previews. Both actual PNGs were opened
and inspected; all six export hashes, page geometry and embedded fonts passed
machine QA separately. The figures show complete net reactions in the forward
direction, not invented mechanistic arrows or experimentally observed states.

Cases include pyridine acid-chloride formation (US7834039B2, GeneralF),
benzophenone phenolic carbamoylation (US7279470B2, Example193), racemic
serinamide primary-O mesylation (US5650428, Example72), and two complex
quinoline ether preparations (US7576215B2, Examples500/525).
The quinoline examples preserve the C3 benzoyl-versus-benzyl distinction,
C8-CF3, linker length, both aryl ethers and the retained methyl ester.

Each case includes its actual frozen generated occurrence/source hashes and
primary preparation locator. Short display conditions are accompanied by
`source_conditions_full` in the figure manifest and the complete bound source
registry; source heading errors and unavailable individual yields are explicit.
No yield is transferred from a referenced general-method example. Selection
includes every new positive case, not only MCTS; it cannot estimate population
chemical accuracy. No model/GPU task or old figure was changed.
